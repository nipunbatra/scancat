Your webcam is a scanner.

Hold a page up to the camera. Scancat finds its edges, straightens it, cleans it up the way a flatbed would, and stacks page after page into one PDF. No phone, no AirDrop, no cropping by hand. Everything runs in this tab; nothing is uploaded.

    Captured pages stack up here. Click a page to rotate it, fix its corners, or delete it; drag to reorder.

    edges found live

    The camera feed is scanned a dozen times a second for the four straight edges of a page — a hand over one edge is fine. Hold still for a moment and the page is captured on its own; or press space whenever you like. Corners can be nudged afterwards.

    cleaned like a scanner

    The page is un-skewed with a perspective warp, then uneven lighting is flattened so paper reads as flat white and ink as ink. Pick colour, greyscale, or crisp black & white — and rotate any page that came in sideways.

    local by design

    A strict Content-Security-Policy forbids every network request, so the page couldn't upload a frame even if it wanted to. The PDF is written in this tab, from scratch. Open source, MIT.
